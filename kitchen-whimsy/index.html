Kitchen Whimsy

A playful little kitchen on your phone. Open the fridge to see what's inside, get reminded before food goes off, keep your shopping list on the fridge door and your favourite recipes in a recipe book. Waste less, cook more.

Not in the store yet
Google Play

A kitchen, not a list

Your home screen is a drawn kitchen. Tap the fridge to open it, the bin to see what was thrown away, the lamp to switch between light and dark.

Expiry reminders

Add what you buy with an emoji and an expiry date guessed from its name. A daily reminder tells you what to use up first.

A note on the fridge

Your shopping list hangs on the fridge door. Things you use up land on it by themselves, and bought items move straight into the fridge.

Your own recipe book

Write down the dishes you make, with a photo if you like. See which ones you can cook with what's in the fridge, and add what's missing to your list.

Learn from the bin

Watch thrown-away food fly into the bin, then see what you waste most each week and month, with a tip on how to waste less.

Private by design

No account, no ads. Everything stays on your phone and works offline, in English and Turkish.
