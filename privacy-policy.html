Privacy Policy

Last updated: Aug 10, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Oopsy Loopsy ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

By using Oopsy Loopsy, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you disagree with any part of this policy, please do not use our application.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

Oopsy Loopsy is designed to work entirely on your device. The information you provide within the app includes:

  • Habit names, descriptions, colors, and icons you create
  • Habit completion logs and check-in history
  • To-do items, categories, priorities, and due dates
  • Reminder times and notification preferences
  • App appearance preferences (light/dark mode)

All of this data is stored locally on your device using a local database. We do not transmit this data to our servers.

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

If you have enabled advertisements within the app, our advertising partner (Google AdMob) may automatically collect certain information, including:

  • Device identifiers (Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location
  • Device type, operating system, and version
  • App usage data and interaction with ads

This data is collected by Google AdMob to serve relevant advertisements. We do not have direct access to this data. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for more information.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information stored on your device solely to:

  • Provide and maintain the core functionality of the app
  • Display your habits, tasks, streaks, and statistics
  • Send you local notifications for habit reminders you have set
  • Remember your app preferences such as theme settings

We do not use your personal habit or task data for any commercial purpose, analytics, or advertising targeting.

4. Data Storage and Security

All your habit and task data is stored locally on your device using a secure local database (Room/SQLite). This means:

  • Your data never leaves your device unless you choose to export it
  • We do not have access to your personal habit or task information
  • Uninstalling the app will permanently delete all locally stored data
  • We recommend enabling device-level security (PIN, fingerprint) to protect your data

While we implement security measures within the app, please be aware that no method of electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Third-Party Services

5.1 Google AdMob

Our app may display advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob uses cookies and device identifiers to serve ads based on your prior visits to our app and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device settings:

Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization

For more information, please visit Google’s Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy

5.2 Firebase Analytics

Oopsy Loopsy uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how users interact with the application and to improve the app's functionality and user experience.

Firebase Analytics may automatically collect certain information, including:

  • App usage and interaction data
  • Automatically collected events such as app launches, sessions, and basic engagement
  • Device and app information
  • Approximate location information
  • App instance identifiers and other technical identifiers

We do not intentionally collect or send your personal habit names, task content, mood entries, descriptions, reminder content, or other personal data to Firebase Analytics. We do not create custom analytics events or custom user properties containing your personal habit, task, or mood information.

Firebase Analytics processes this information to provide aggregated analytics and usage insights. We do not use your personal habit, task, or mood data for analytics or advertising purposes. For more information about how Google Analytics for Firebase collects and processes data, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Children’s Privacy

Oopsy Loopsy is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected personal information from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete such information.

7. Your Rights

Since all your data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it at all times. You can:

  • Access all your data directly within the app
  • Delete individual habits, tasks, or logs within the app
  • Delete all app data by uninstalling the application
  • Opt out of personalized ads through your device settings

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have certain data protection rights under GDPR. Since we do not collect or process your personal habit data on our servers, most GDPR obligations are fulfilled by the local-only storage model.

8. Data Retention

Your habit and task data is retained on your device for as long as you use the application. Upon uninstalling the app, all locally stored data is permanently deleted. We do not retain any copies of your data on our servers.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy and, where appropriate, displaying a notice within the app.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes. Changes to this Privacy Policy are effective when they are posted.
