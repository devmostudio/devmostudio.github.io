Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data secure?

Yes. We use a local SQLite/Room database. We don't have servers, so we can't see your habits or tasks.

How can I backup my data?

Since the app works offline, your data is backed up via standard Android System Backups if enabled on your device.

Does the app collect analytics?

We use Firebase Analytics to improve app performance, but we never collect your personal habit or task titles.
